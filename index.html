Unsubstantiated Return Claim

SEC Rule 156

Prohibits investment companies from making materially misleading statements in sales literature. Performance guarantees without substantiation violate this rule directly.
High Risk

Flagged Phrase

“Guaranteed 15% Returns.”

Suggested Replacement

Misleading Performance Statement

FINRA Rule 2210

Requires all communications to be fair, balanced, and not misleading. Statements implying certainty of future performance are explicitly prohibited.
High Risk

Flagged Phrase

“Past performance guarantees future results”

Suggested Replacement

“Past performance is not indicative of future results. Investing involves risk, including possible loss of principal.”

Missing Required Disclosures

SEC Rule 206(4)-1

Investment advisers must include clear disclosure of their registered status and advisory relationship in any advertisement directed at prospective clients.
